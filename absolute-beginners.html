The Polish Alphabet & Pronunciation

Got questions?

Here are the most common ones for absolute beginners.

Do I need to know anything before starting?

No! That's the point. Absolute Beginners is for people with zero experience. You don't need to know a single word.

How long does it take to have a conversation?

It depends on you, but most students can hold basic conversations within 2-3 months of regular lessons. Some faster, some slower — we go at your pace.

What if I'm nervous or shy about speaking?

That's completely normal. My lessons are patient, supportive, and pressure-free. We start with the absolute basics and build your confidence slowly.

Is Polish really that hard?

It's different, not impossible. Once you understand the patterns (and have a native speaker explain them), it starts making sense. I promise.
