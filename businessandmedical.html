Questions from professionals

Everything you need to know about specialised Polish training.

What level do I need to start?

For medical and business Polish, I recommend at least A2 level. This ensures you have the basic grammar and vocabulary foundation to build on. If you're below A2, we can work on general Polish first.

Can you tailor lessons to my specific field?

Absolutely. Every lesson is tailored to your profession, your specific role, and the situations you face daily. I'll work with you to identify exactly what you need.

Do you work with doctors who need to pass the language exam?

Yes. Many medical professionals need B1 level for Polish citizenship or B2/C1 for medical registration. We can prepare you for both the exam and real-world clinical communication.

How long before I notice a difference?

Most professionals notice improved confidence within 4-6 weeks of weekly lessons. By 3 months, you'll be handling professional conversations and documents with much greater ease.

Do you also teach Polish business etiquette?

Yes! Language and culture go hand in hand. I'll teach you the unspoken rules of Polish workplace culture, formal communication styles, and how to navigate professional relationships.
