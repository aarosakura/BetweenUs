Why learn Polish for your trip?

A few words go a long way. Here's what changes when you learn.

🍽️

Order Food Like a Local

Skip the tourist menus. Order pierogi, żurek, and oscypek in Polish — and discover dishes that aren't translated for visitors.

🗺️

Navigate with Confidence

Read signs, ask for directions, understand train schedules, and travel independently — even in smaller towns where English isn't spoken.

🤝

Connect with Locals

Poles appreciate when you try. A simple "Dzień dobry" or "Dziękuję" opens hearts. You'll leave with friends, not just photos.

🏛️

Understand the Culture

Poland's history and culture come alive when you understand the language. Museums, tours, and conversations take on new meaning.

🎁

Shop & Bargain

Visit local markets, ask for prices, and maybe even haggle a little. You'll find better deals and more authentic experiences.

What you'll learn

Practical Polish for every part of your journey.

👋

Essential Travel Phrases

Greetings, thank yous, please, excuse me, and all the basics you need to be polite and well-received everywhere.

🏨

Hotels & Accommodation

Checking in, asking for amenities, reporting issues, and checking out — confidently handle every hotel interaction.

🚆

Transport & Getting Around

Trains, buses, trams, taxis — understand timetables, buy tickets, and ask for the right platform or stop.

🍲

Ordering Food & Drinks

Read menus, order confidently, ask for recommendations, and handle dietary requirements — all in Polish.

🛍️

Shopping & Markets

Ask for prices, describe what you're looking for, and shop like a local at Poland's beautiful markets.

🚑

Emergencies & Healthcare

Basic medical vocabulary, asking for help, explaining symptoms, and navigating pharmacies — just in case.

🎭

Cultural Etiquette & Customs

Learn how Poles greet, what to say at the table, how to show respect, and the do's and don'ts of Polish culture.

🇵🇱

Polish History & Traditions

Understand the significance of places, holidays, and traditions — your travels become richer when you know the story behind them.

Beyond words: Polish culture

Learning the language means understanding the culture. Here's what makes Poland special.

🥟

Food & Feast Traditions

From pierogi to bigos, from Christmas Eve wigilia to Easter breakfast — Polish food is a journey in itself. Learn what to order and how to eat it.

🎶

Music, Art & Festivals

Chopin, folk music, open-air festivals, and vibrant city culture. Poland's artistic soul is everywhere — and it's beautiful.

🏰

History & Architecture

Medieval castles, Gothic churches, Renaissance towns, and Soviet-era relics — Poland's history is written in stone. Understand what you're seeing.

🤗

Polish Hospitality

Poles are some of the warmest hosts in the world — once you earn their trust. Learn the customs and you'll be welcomed like family.

Got questions?

Everything you need to know about learning Polish for travel.

Do I need to know any Polish before starting?

No! This course is designed for absolute beginners. We start from zero and focus on the phrases you'll actually use while traveling.

How many lessons do I need before my trip?

It depends on how long your trip is and how much you want to learn. Most students take 4-8 lessons before traveling to feel confident with the basics.

Will I learn about Polish culture too?

Absolutely! Language and culture go hand in hand. I'll share insights about Polish customs, traditions, food, and history — so you can travel with understanding.

Can I understand menus and signs after these lessons?

Yes! We'll focus on practical reading skills — menus, signs, timetables, and more. You'll be able to navigate confidently.
